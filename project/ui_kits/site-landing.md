The whole landing page, assembled from the landing kits in the order of the approved page: pilot bar, header, hero with the Ask Batsi peek, Modules, Ask Batsi, Recruitment, Onboarding and Offboarding, Compliance Management, Leave Management, Trust and POPIA, Integrations, Platform, About, the closing band and the footer. ## How the page joins up

- **The menu and the sheet point at the sections**, as on the approved page. Ask Batsi, Recruitment, Onboarding, Leave, Compliance, Platform, Integrations, Trust, Company and Book a demo scroll to their section. Links to pages that are not built yet (Employee Records, HR Help Desk, Resources, Log in and the Learn more pages) keep the approved page's addresses.
- **MCP server and Events** scroll to Integrations and switch it to that tab.
- **The header** turns frosted and shorter once the page has scrolled 24px, as in the Site header kit. At 390px the menu button opens the sheet over the page.
- **Ids end in -d or -p** (for example `#recruitment-d`) because both widths sit on one kit page. On the site each id is used once, without the ending.
