Recruitment

Every candidate tracked from application to signed offer.

One hiring board per role. Batsi watches each stage for candidates who go quiet and drafts the follow-up for HR to send.

  • Six hiring stages

    Applied, Screening, Interview, Offer, Hired and Not hired. Every move recorded with a reason.

  • Interview panels

    Panel members score against the same criteria. No candidate is rejected automatically.

  • Reminders and follow-ups

    Interviewers reminded to submit feedback. Candidates reminded to reply or upload.

    Batsi

  • Candidate risk

    At-risk flags for no reply, missed interviews and offers close to expiry.

    Batsi

Compliance Management

Every certificate tracked to its expiry date.

Licences, certificates and permits are recorded against the role that needs them. Reminders go out at 60, 30 and 7 days. Anyone with an expired requirement is listed as not cleared to work.

  • Requirements per role

    Each role lists the licences, certificates and permits it needs. New employees inherit them on day one.

  • Reminders at 60, 30 and 7 days

    Sent to the employee and the manager before anything expires. Overdue items go to HR.

    Batsi

  • Renewals read

    Batsi reads the renewed certificate and suggests the new expiry date. HR accepts it.

    Batsi

  • Not cleared to work

    One list of everyone with an expired requirement, updated the moment a renewal is accepted.

Ask Batsi

Ask Batsi about any employee or candidate.

Batsi answers from the employee record, reads documents and drafts the follow-up. Every action waits for HR to approve it.

Trust and POPIA

Every action approved. Every step recorded.

Batsi suggests and drafts. HR approves before anything is sent or saved. Each step goes into the audit log with a name, a time and whether a person or Batsi did it. All data stays in South Africa.
