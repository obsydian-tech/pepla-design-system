Batsi reads every document. HR makes every decision.

Naleko People reads ID documents, qualifications and bank letters as they are uploaded, fills the employee record and checks each detail against it. HR accepts the document or asks for a new one.

  • 13-digit ID number present, date of birth matches it
  • Gender and citizenship digits valid
  • Name and surname match the record
  • Bank letter matches the account holder
  • Matches, Needs a look or Doesn't match, with the reasonBatsi

Contract and probation

Contracts come from the company template and are signed online.

Naleko People fills the contract from the employee record, sends it for signature and stores the signed copy on the record. Probation dates are set at the same time.

  • Contract templates per company and role
  • Details filled from the record, no retyping
  • Signed online, signed copy stored on the record
  • Salary hidden on screen
  • Probation end date with a reminder to confirm or extend probation
