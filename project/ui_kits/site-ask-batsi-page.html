Documents read and matched

Batsi reads every document. HR makes every decision.

Naleko People reads ID documents, qualifications and bank letters as they are uploaded, fills the employee record and checks each detail against it. HR accepts the document or asks for a new one.

  • 13-digit ID number present, date of birth matches it
  • Gender and citizenship digits valid
  • Name and surname match the record
  • Bank letter matches the account holder
  • Matches, Needs a look or Doesn't match, with the reasonBatsi
  • Every decision logged with who made it

Approvals

A person approves every action.

Batsi suggests, drafts and reminds. Nothing is sent, accepted or changed until HR approves it. Writes that arrive through the API or the MCP server wait in the same approval queue.

  • Batsi drafts, a person approvesBatsi
  • Nothing sent or saved before approval
  • API and MCP writes use the same queue
  • Approver recorded with a name and a time
  • Rejected actions logged with the reason

Audit trail

Every step recorded, and exported in one step.

Every upload, check, approval and view goes into the audit log. Each entry shows who did it, when, and whether it was a person or Batsi.

  • Name, time and what happened on every entry
  • Person or Batsi shown on each entry
  • Views of personal data logged
  • Export to CSV for an audit
  • Entries kept with the name of who made them

Limits

Batsi stays on HR work.

Batsi answers questions about employees, candidates and documents. Salary benchmarks, employment law advice and individual performance ratings are not given, and requests outside HR work are declined.

  • Questions about employees, candidates and documents answered
  • Salary benchmarks not given
  • Employment law advice not given
  • Individual performance ratings not given
  • Requests outside HR work declined
