Hiring board

One hiring board per role, six stages.

Applied, Screening, Interview, Offer, Hired and Not hired. Candidates move across the board and every move is recorded with a reason.

  • One board for each open role
  • Every move recorded with who made it and why
  • Not hired always carries a reason
  • Candidate details open from the card, sensitive data hidden
  • Counts per stage update as candidates move

Interview panels

Every panel member scores against the same criteria.

Panel members score each candidate on the criteria set for the role. Scores are combined on one sheet. Scores inform the decision. They never make it.

  • Scoring criteria set per role
  • Each panel member scores on their own
  • Average per criterion and overall
  • No candidate is rejected automatically
  • Decision recorded with the name of the person who made it

Reminders and follow-ups

Interviewers and candidates are reminded on schedule.

Batsi chases missing interview feedback and drafts the reminder for a candidate who has gone quiet. HR sends it.

  • Interviewers reminded to submit feedbackBatsi
  • Candidates reminded to reply, confirm or uploadBatsi
  • Reminder drafted for HR to edit and send
  • Every reminder logged on the candidate

Candidate risk

Candidates at risk of dropping out are flagged early.

Batsi flags candidates who go quiet, miss an interview or have an offer close to expiry. Each flag shows the reason and a suggested next step for HR.

  • No reply for a set number of daysBatsi
  • Missed or unconfirmed interviewsBatsi
  • Offers close to expiryBatsi
  • Flags are advice. HR decides every action
  • Thresholds set per company

Offer to onboarding

A signed offer starts onboarding with no retyping.

When an offer is signed, the candidate moves to Hired and an employee record is created with the details already captured. Onboarding opens and HR is told.

  • Offer letter from the company template
  • Offer expiry tracked and reminded
  • Name, role and start date carried to the employee record
  • Salary stays hidden on screen
  • Onboarding opens on its own
