Modules

Modules switched on for each company.

Employee Records come with every company. Recruitment, Onboarding and Offboarding, Leave Management and Compliance Management are switched on by Naleko after the onboarding call. The sidebar follows the settings.

  • Employee Records with every company
  • Other modules switched on by Naleko
  • Sidebar and Today list follow the modules
  • HR Help Desk and Performance Management coming soon
  • Each change logged

Templates and checklists

Hiring stages and checklists set in Settings.

Hiring stages, onboarding checklists and document lists are templates. HR edits them in Settings, without code, and new hires get the current version.

  • Hiring stages set per company
  • Onboarding checklists and document lists as templates
  • Edited in Settings, without code
  • New hires get the current template
  • Every template change logged

Leave types and holidays

South African defaults. Company changes in Settings.

BCEA leave types and South African public holidays are ready on day one. Companies add their own leave types and days off in Settings.

  • BCEA leave types ready on day one
  • South African public holidays included
  • Company leave types added in Settings
  • Company days off added to the team calendar

Roles and access

Each role sees its own modules and records.

There are five roles: Administrator, HR, Manager, Employee and Guest. Access follows the role and the modules switched on for the company.

  • Administrator, HR, Manager, Employee and Guest
  • Each role sees only its own modules and records
  • Permissions added to a role for Recruitment, IT and offers
  • Access changes logged
