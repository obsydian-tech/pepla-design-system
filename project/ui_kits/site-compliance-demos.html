Requirements per role

Each role lists the certificates it needs.

Licences, certificates and permits are recorded against the role that needs them. New employees inherit the requirements on day one, and they are collected during onboarding.

  • Requirements set per role and per site
  • New employees inherit them automatically
  • Collected during onboarding, straight onto the register
  • Expiry date recorded for each certificate
  • Requirement changes applied to everyone in the role

Reminders

Reminders go out at 60, 30 and 7 days.

Renewals read

Batsi reads the renewal. HR accepts the new date.

When an employee uploads a renewed certificate, Batsi reads it, checks the holder against the record and suggests the new expiry date. HR accepts the certificate or asks for a new one.

  • Holder, certificate type, issue and expiry dates readBatsi
  • Holder checked against the employee record
  • Matches, Needs a look or Doesn't match, with the reason
  • Register updated when HR accepts
  • Every decision logged with who made it

Not cleared to work

Anyone with an expired requirement is listed.

When a requirement expires, the employee joins the not cleared to work list and HR and the manager are told. The list empties when the renewal is accepted.

  • One list of everyone not cleared to work
  • HR and the manager told the day a requirement expires
  • Reason shown against each employee
  • Removed from the list once the renewal is accepted
  • Every change logged
