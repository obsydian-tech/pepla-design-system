# Site pages

The eight site pages besides the landing page, each assembled whole from the site kits and shown at desktop width and at 390px. A Learn more page has the header, the page top with its hero card, the sub-menu, the page's demo sections, the Ask Batsi band, the trust strip, "Works together with", the questions, the closing band and the footer. Book a demo and Get started have the header, the form page top, the closing band and the footer.

## Screens

- **Recruitment page**. Hiring board, Interview panels, Reminders and follow-ups, Candidate risk, Offer to onboarding.
- **Onboarding page**. New employee's side, Documents read and matched, Follow-ups, Contract and probation, Laptop and access, Offboarding.
- **Compliance page**. Requirements per role, Certificate register, Reminders, Renewals read, Not cleared to work.
- **Leave page**. Leave types, Requests, Approvals, Balances and calendar.
- **Security page**. Approvals, Audit trail, Hidden data, POPIA consent, Data in South Africa.
- **Platform page**. Modules, Templates and checklists, Leave types and holidays, Roles and access.
- **Book a demo**. The form page top with the demo request form.
- **Get started**. The form page top with the company account form.

Uses Site header, Site menu, Site sheet, Site hero, Site form page, Site hero card, Site sub-menu, Site demo, Site Ask Batsi, Site trust strip, Site related modules, Site module cards, Site questions, Site closing band and Site footer.

## Where the redraw changes the approved site

See `DEVIATIONS.md` for the full table. In short:

- **Section ids carry the page and width** (`#hiring-board-red`), because all sixteen pages sit on one kit page. The site uses each id once.
- **The sub-menu tab "Masked data" reads "Hidden data"**, by the words rule.
- **Links to other pages point at `#`** on the kit page. The Angular site links the real pages.
