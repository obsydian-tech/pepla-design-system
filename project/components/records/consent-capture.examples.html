How Mokoena Accounting uses your information Before you upload documents, here is what we keep and why. You can read the full notice at any time.
What we collect
Your ID number, contact details, bank account, tax number and an emergency contact.
Why
To pay you, to register you with SARS and UIF, and to reach someone if you are hurt at work.
Who receives it
The HR team at Mokoena Accounting. The payroll system (South Africa) gets your name, ID, bank and tax details. SARS gets your tax details. Pepla stores it all in Cape Town.
How long we keep it
While you work here, and 5 years after you leave. Then it is deleted.
